How it works

From application to your first payment.

Joining Payset Private is a conversation, not a form. Here is what happens after you apply.

Apply now
  1. 015 minutes

    Apply

    Tell us about your company, your monthly volume, your currencies and where you pay and get paid. If a client introduced you, say who.

  2. 02Within one business day

    A conversation

    A senior member of our team calls you. We want to understand how your business moves money, and you should know exactly what we can and can’t do.

  3. 03After the call

    Your proposal

    A plan built around your volume and corridors: monthly fee, FX pricing and service level, in writing.

  4. 04[ONBOARDING TIME]

    Onboarding, led by people

    Your account manager collects what compliance needs once, explains every request and keeps it moving. Then your accounts open.

Questions.

Can I open an account online?

No. Every client starts with a conversation, so we can confirm we’re the right fit before you share documents.

What if we move less than six figures a month?

Apply anyway if you’re growing quickly. If we’re not the right fit, we’ll tell you on the call.

Do you work with groups with many entities?

Yes. Each entity gets its own accounts, all under one account manager.

Is Payset a bank?

No. Payset is an Electronic Money Institution authorised by the FCA. Client funds are safeguarded, held separately from our own and never lent out.
